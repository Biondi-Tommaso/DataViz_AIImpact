A data story

Mathematics is being rewritten.

For centuries, math moved at the speed of human thought. Artificial intelligence is changing that pace. We built this page to show how, with data.

Begin the story
Chapter 1 · Why it matters

Everything runs on math.

Encryption, medicine, climate models, the chip in your phone: all of it rests on equations and proofs. Progress used to depend on a few brilliant minds and a great deal of time. Now machines have joined the conversation, and the impact on mathematics is enormous.

Chapter 2 · Research

AI is becoming a research partner.

It has spotted hidden patterns in knot theory, found faster ways to multiply matrices and solved olympiad problems once thought out of reach. Mathematicians are no longer working alone.

Chapter 3 · The price of progress

But this power is not free.

Frontier models need thousands of chips, vast amounts of energy and budgets that only a handful of organizations can afford. Who gets to do AI-powered mathematics tomorrow?

Chapter 4 · What comes next

A new chapter, not the last.

Proofs verified by computers, AI tutors for students, open questions about trust and credit. The story of AI and mathematics is still being written, and you can be part of it.
