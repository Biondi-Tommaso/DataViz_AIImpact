A night walk · A data story

Mathematics is being rewritten.

For centuries, math moved at the speed of human thought. Artificial intelligence is changing that pace. Climb the hill with us: three statues wait at the top, each telling one part of the story.

Begin the walk
Why it matters

Everything runs on math.

Encryption, medicine, climate models, the chip in your phone: all of it rests on equations and proofs. Progress used to depend on a few brilliant minds and a great deal of time. Now machines have joined the conversation, and the impact is enormous. Let's meet the three statues that tell its story.

Meet the first statue
Statue I · Research

AI is becoming a research partner.

It has spotted hidden patterns in knot theory, found faster ways to multiply matrices and solved olympiad problems once thought out of reach. Mathematicians are no longer working alone.

Statue II · Cost

But this power is not free.

Frontier models need thousands of chips, vast amounts of energy and budgets that only a handful of organizations can afford. Who gets to do AI-powered mathematics tomorrow?

Statue III · Mind

And then there's the human side.

Trust, creativity, identity. When a machine proves a theorem, what happens to the people who have spent their lives chasing proofs? The most overlooked impact of AI may be a psychological one.
