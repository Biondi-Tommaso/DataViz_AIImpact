2019–2022: first steps

Before the Lean 4 breakthrough

Between 2019 and 2022, neural theorem proving was fragmented across Isabelle, Coq, HOL Light, and Metamath; works based on Lean and Mathlib were still a minority.

2023–2026: acceleration

The rise of Lean 4 and Mathlib4

With Lean 4, Mathlib4, and new LLM provers (AlphaProof, DeepSeek-Prover, Goedel-Prover), Lean is rapidly becoming the dominant language in AI-assisted formal verification.
